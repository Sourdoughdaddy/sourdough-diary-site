How it is worked out

A tenth extra

The amounts include a tenth more than you asked for, for what sticks to the jar and the spoon.

More food, more time

Time to peak grows with the square root of the food: four times the food takes about twice as long.

Warm is fast

Fermentation roughly doubles in speed for every 10°C. A warm kitchen shortens the wait and a cool one stretches it.

This is an estimate from one number about your starter. The app does the same sum from your own feedings and corrects itself each time you tell it the jar has peaked.
