Every loaf, written down

A diary of everything you have baked.

The Pinnwand

Each finished loaf, pinned up as a polaroid. On an iPad it is a whole cork wall.

Front and back

The loaf on the front, the crumb on the back.

Tip the phone and the photos on the wall swing. Turn one over for the crumb shot, or send it to someone as a card.

Your year

A calendar of every feeding and every loaf.

Streaks, your best month, and how many loaves you are on pace for.

Numbers that stay honest

What your best loaves have in common.

When Stats says something about your baking, it has checked first that the pattern isn't chance. When it can't tell, it says so.

Stickers you earn

Twenty-six badges, and a new challenge every month. Each one is a sticker for a loaf.

Madame Crumb will see you now

A fortune for every loaf, and a deck to collect.

Her tent stands on the Pinnwand. Add a crumb shot and she deals the cards. There are twelve to find, and the tent changes with the season.
